National student achievement

June 2026

Top 10 in the nation in Microsoft Excel

Recent Butler graduate and BTA major Seth Raymond ’26 represented Indiana and Butler at the Microsoft Office Specialist U.S. National Championship in Nashville and finished in the Top 10 nationally. He reached the national stage after earning a perfect 1000/1000 on his Excel certification exam and becoming Indiana State Champion. Seth was also active in MISTA during his Butler career.

Statewide analytics leadership

Spring 2026

Butler helps bring Indiana together through the Final Four Analytics Challenge

The NCAA Men's Final Four Analytics Challenge has become Indiana's premier collegiate sports-analytics competition. Butler again demonstrated leadership by helping organize and lead the statewide event, bringing students from NCAA institutions across Indiana together around a common analytics problem. The 2026 kickoff drew 751 students from 23 Indiana schools, and Butler's undergraduate team of Max Feltner, Aman Kikani, Tucker Klancnik, and Lucas Krol finished 4th in the undergraduate division.

Data4Good

2025 competition · recognized in 2026

16 of the top 17 Midwest undergraduate teams came from Butler

Butler's MS 365 Predictive Analytics students produced a remarkable regional showing in the 2025 National Data4Good Analytics Competition: 16 of the top 17 undergraduate teams in the Midwest were Butler teams. Jack Maier and Mariah Richardson advanced as Regional Champions, with members of the regional team earning a $2,000 prize. Their project used predictive analytics to address AI factuality and misinformation in education.

Faculty advisor recognition

October 2025

Dr. Matthew Lanham receives inaugural INFORMS Data Mining Society Teaching Award

Faculty co-advisor Dr. Matthew Lanham received the 2025 Inaugural INFORMS Data Mining Society Teaching Award, recognizing his impact on data science and analytics education inside and beyond the classroom. The recognition reinforces the chapter's connection to the broader INFORMS professional community and its emphasis on experiential, outcomes-based analytics education.

National faculty recognition

December 2025

Poets&Quants names Dr. Lanham one of the 50 Best Undergraduate Business Professors

Poets&Quants for Undergrads selected Dr. Matthew Lanham as one of its 50 Best Undergraduate Business School Professors of 2025. The national honor emphasized his experiential approach to analytics education, including embedding competitions and applied work directly into courses so students leave with tangible evidence of competence and confidence.

AI prototyping

December 2025

Students build working app prototypes in under an hour

Butler's first Vibe Coding workshop and competition, organized through collaboration among MISTA, the Butler INFORMS Student Chapter, faculty, and entrepreneurship partners, showed how quickly students can move from an idea to a working prototype using modern AI-assisted development tools. Students—many with little or no coding background—built applications in under 60 minutes. A debt-management application created by Preston Asher earned first place.
