Vision Zero Fieldwork: Analytics in the Community

46th Street near the Monon TrailCity of Indianapolis DPWHealth by Design

Business Technology & Analytics students in Dr. Jason Davidson's Data Analytics & Business Modeling class partnered with the City of Indianapolis Department of Public Works and Health by Design to support Vision Zero. Students collected field data around a temporary street-design project, examining how changes such as barriers, painted bike lanes, and street murals may affect drivers, cyclists, and pedestrians.

Business Technology & AnalyticsExperiential learningSports analytics

Communicating with Purpose: How to Make Your Research Understandable and Valuable

9:00–9:30 AMPharmacy & Health Sciences 150Poster presentations 1:45–3:15 PM

Clear communication is essential for research to have influence beyond an academic setting. The session focused on presenting complex ideas in simple language, identifying a project's main message, and explaining value to a general audience. Students from MS 365 Predictive Analytics also presented their posters at the Butler Undergraduate Research Conference.

Speaker: Matthew Lanham, Assistant Professor of Business Technology & Analytics, Butler University.
